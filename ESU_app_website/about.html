About us

Make billing boring again.

Boring as in predictable. Clear. Nothing hiding at the bottom of the receipt. That's the whole mission.

Why we started

Every one of us had paid a bill we didn't fully understand — and walked away because asking felt awkward.

PayRight began as a student entrepreneurship project. We noticed the same pattern at restaurants, cafés and delivery apps: a line we didn't recognise, a total that felt off, and no easy way to check.

Consumers don't lack rights. They lack time, information and evidence at the exact moment they need it. So we built a tool that gives all three — in the eight seconds before you pay.

What we believe

Four rules we build by.

01

Show the working.

Every number comes with its calculation. If we can't explain it, we don't show it.

02

Unusual ≠ illegal.

We separate maths from rules from opinion, and we say “we're not sure” when we aren't.

03

Fair to merchants too.

Merchant profiles use verified receipts, moderation and a right to respond — never raw complaint counts.

04

Your data is yours.

Receipts stay private. We don't sell personal receipt or complaint data. Ever.
