Legal

The fine print, not so fine.

Written to be read — plain language, no surprises.

What we collect

  • Account details you give us — name, email.
  • Receipts you scan and the fields extracted from them.
  • Corrections you make, groups you create and settlements you record.

How we use it

To audit your bills, keep your history, split expenses and improve extraction accuracy. Receipts are stored privately and accessed through short-lived links.

What we never do

  • Sell identifiable receipt or complaint data.
  • Show your personal transactions on public merchant pages.
  • Share evidence with a merchant or authority without your explicit action.

Your controls

Export your data or delete your account at any time from Settings in the app. Deleted receipts are removed from active systems.

Using PayRight

PayRight provides bill analysis, expense tools and general consumer-rights information. You're responsible for the receipts you upload and for any messages you choose to send.

Accuracy

Extraction may contain errors. You can review and correct every field before an audit is finalised. Arithmetic checks are deterministic; rule-based guidance depends on the rule version shown in each report.

Payments

PayRight does not move money between users. Settlements are records you create.

Fair use

Don't upload receipts that aren't yours, submit false reports about merchants, or misuse the complaint tools.

Not legal advice

PayRight offers legal information, not individual legal advice. It is not a law firm, statutory authority, regulator, payment institution or government-approved enforcement agency.

No accusations

A flagged charge is not a finding that a business acted unlawfully. Labels such as “Review” or “Unexplained” mean a question is worth asking.
